    Controls

    Keyboard and mouse

    WASD
    Walk (up is where the camera is looking)
    W W
    Run: tap W twice (you keep running while you hold it)
    Shift
    Crouch (one tap; another, stand up). Crouching you walk slowly, and what you place doesn't join what's next to it. Swimming, you sink a little
    Left click
    Use what you're holding: chop, mine, water, sow, fish, attack, eat, place… Hold to keep going.
    Space
    Jump (with a dialogue open, continue). Swimming, rise higher; next to the shore, jump out
    E or Enter
    Interact with what you point at with the mouse (2 tiles away at most; pointing at nothing, with the nearest thing): talk, doors, chests, stations, sleep, harvest, animals
    1–9 or wheel
    Choose an item
    R
    Rotate what you're about to place (aim with the mouse: up to 4 tiles). Held, it keeps turning; tapped, in 15° steps, snapping to the tiles
    ← / →
    Turn the camera around you (hold: smooth turn)
    ↑ / ↓
    Raise the camera until you almost see from above, or lower it to ground level
    + / − or Ctrl+wheel
    Zoom the camera in and out
    Z
    First or third person (in first person, ↑ ↓ look up and down)
    F
    Show performance (frames per second)
    I C B
    Inventory (what you carry and your clothes), crafting and building
    Tab
    While you hold it, who is playing
    J M
    Journal and map
    T or /
    Chat and commands (/help to see them). Ops (given in the server terminal with /op) have /gamemode, /tp, /give, /kill, /weather, /gamerule…; the admin also has /account, to create accounts
    Esc
    Pause (and close windows)

    Open it with the launcher

    The game needs a small local server to work. Double-click jugar.command (in the game's folder) and it will open in the browser by itself.

    You can also run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder and go to http://localhost:8000
