    Keyboard and mouse

    WASD
    Walk (up is where the camera is looking)
    W W · W W W
    Jog: tap W, then press it again and hold it (it uses no energy). Sprint: tap W twice and hold it on the third press (it drains the energy bar; out of breath, you drop to a jog). Let go of W and you stop; the next press walks again. Riding, one press walks, the second canters (it trots on the way up) and the third gallops; swimming, a steady crawl and a fast swim
    Shift
    Posture. A tap: crouch, or stand up if you're crouching. Hold it: sit on the ground; sitting, hold it again to lie face up (a tap brings you up one step: lying, you sit up; sitting, you crouch). Walking gets you up. While crouching you move slowly and what you place doesn't connect to its neighbors. While swimming, Shift dives: hold it to go down, and Space to come up
    Y or right click
    Greet the person under the cursor or in front of you (a neighbor, your child, another player); hold it to provoke them instead. The village notices: your reputation is in the journal
    Left click
    Use what you're holding: chop, mine, water, sow, fish, attack, eat, place… Hold to keep going.
    Space
    Jump (in a dialogue: continue). While swimming, hold it to come up; at the surface next to the shore, climb out
    E or Enter
    Interact with what you point at with the mouse (2 tiles away at most; pointing at nothing, with the nearest thing): talk, doors, chests, stations, sleep, harvest, animals. What each key does shows at the thing; a key with a ring has to be held
    1–9, wheel and E
    Talking to a neighbor, the world goes on: pick what to do from the list with the number keys, the mouse wheel or the arrows and E, or the mouse. E skips what is being said, Esc goes back or says goodbye, and walking away ends it. Ask… tells you what's new, where somebody is, how to get to a place you know, some place you haven't seen (from 1 heart; a circle stays on the map) and tomorrow's weather. Stand near two neighbors who are chatting and you overhear them; what people say about you is in the journal, Neighbors
    E on water
    Thirsty, aim at a river, a lake or a well to drink (one press drinks until you're full; moving stops it). Sea water is too salty. With a canteen or a jug in hand: click for a sip, E or click on fresh water to fill it
    Q
    Drop what you hold (with Ctrl or Shift, the whole stack); with something in your arms, put it down
    E Q X
    A felled tree leaves a log and a hunted animal its carcass. E puts it on your shoulder (one at a time) and Q puts it down; X, held, guts a hunted carcass (lighter, and its meat keeps) and then butchers it where it lies, and the axe splits a log. At the bench (E) a log gives 10 planks; at the kitchen (E) a carcass gives the most. Everything weighs: past what you carry comfortably (25 kg, 70 with the backpack) you walk slower
    E Q X
    The wheelbarrow (B › Exterior, its plan at Dani's): E pushes it with both hands (slower, no running, jumping or swimming) and Q parks it; X opens it (12 stacks, which B and C also pay from). Pushing it, E on a log or a carcass loads it (3 at most): what it carries doesn't weigh on you
    E
    Your own business (B › Business): E on the counter opens it, puts goods in and sets their prices; the neighbours buy whether you are there or not, and the coins wait in the till
    Table games
    Sit at a tavern table across a regular (Marta from 17:00, Paco from 19:00), or on the bar's middle stool by day (Eusebio and Inés play Crows), and the window opens: pick the game and the stake. At home put the dice or the tile box on a table. Getting up ends your part. A neighbour plays three games a day with you at most.
    Jobs
    Jobs are on the board's second page and asked for in a talk («Any work for me?»). Your partner joins from J › Jobs. Everyone there at the end is paid in full. Rosa's cattle drive: find the five cows, push them together (they move away from you; on a horse they follow you when you ride calmly away) and drive them into the village's yard.
    E Q
    With a log or a carcass in your arms you can mount your horse (it rides behind the saddle, and the horse is not slowed: Q puts it down beside it) or row a boat (it rides in the bow; if you jump into the water it stays in the boat). Getting off, it's back in your arms
    N
    Whistle for your horse (or your partner's) from a distance: the closer the bond, the farther it hears you
    Riding
    The gallop tires the horse: its bar sits with yours while you ride, and when it is winded it only trots until it gets its breath back. It has weight: it speeds up and stops little by little, the faster it goes the wider it turns, and it slows down for slopes, mud, water and walls. Space jumps a fence with a run-up; without it, it stops. Settings › Game › Riding: Arcade makes it turn and stop more sharply. E with nothing aimed gets off. Brush it, feed it and pat it every day (E on it): a closer bond means a longer gallop, fewer scares and new tricks
    G H riding
    Riding: G pats the horse (and calms it after a scare), H rears (level 3)
    Looking after it
    After a long ride it gets hungry or thirsty and gallops less: stop by fresh water and it drinks, stop on grass and it grazes, or feed it (E on it; its card says how it is). Its shoes wear out: Bruno puts new ones at his forge. Two stumbles in a row leave it sore for a while. In its stable, or by a trough with water, it wakes up well
    Reins
    E on your horse (or your partner's) › Lead by the reins: it follows you on foot. Pointing at nothing, E lets it go; pointing at a hitching post, a fence or a tree, E ties it there, and it waits for you. Untie it with E on it or on the post, or just mount. It won't go through house doors, up cliffs or into deep water, and you need a free hand
    Breeds
    Rosa sells horses of four breeds: the Spanish is good at everything, the Arab fast and long-winded but nervous, the Hispano-Breton strong and calm but slow, and the Asturcón pony small, hardy and sure on slopes. Each horse is a little different (its card says it) and a foal takes after its parents
    Roads
    Dirt roads join the village, the hamlets and the landmarks, and tracks lead from them to the watchtowers, the lighthouses, the temples and the mines. Marked routes follow them, and a shod horse goes a little faster. The signposts say where each way goes (E reads them); on one you build (B, Outside), E chooses what its arms point to among the places you have visited. Bridges and signposts come apart with B, Remove
    X on your horse
    Saddlebags: X on your horse, or riding (12 stacks; B and C also pay from them)
    1–9 or wheel
    Choose an item
    R with the rod or the fish trap
    Change the bait (Shift+R, the other way). The bait makes the rig: the float, the bottom or a lure
    R
    Rotate what you're about to place (aim with the mouse: up to 4 tiles). Held, it keeps turning; tapped, in 15° steps, snapping to the tiles
    ← / →
    Turn the camera around you (hold: smooth turn)
    ↑ / ↓
    Raise the camera until you almost see from above, or lower it to ground level
    + / − or Ctrl+wheel
    Zoom the camera in and out
    Z
    First or third person (in first person, ↑ ↓ look up and down)
    F
    Tool wheel: hold it, move the mouse and let go to equip (playing alone, the game goes in slow motion meanwhile). A tap goes back to what you held before
    F3
    Show performance (frames per second)
    I C B
    Inventory (what you carry and your clothes), crafting and building
    Tab
    While you hold it, who is playing
    X on water
    Muddy, dirty or bloody: aim at a river, a lake or the sea while wading or from the bank to scrub yourself (about 8 s; moving stops it). E on a bathtub takes a bath, and E on a basin washes your face and hands. Soap in your bag washes you fully clean
    The clothes
    Clothes wear out with tool work, sprinting through the woods, lying on the ground, swimming dressed and hits; the bar in their slot shows what is left, and torn clothes keep you less warm. Mend them at the loom (C: one cloth, wool or leather) or for coins with Marta, at the tavern in the evening, who also sells warm clothes by season. The neighbours notice how you look and remember what you do
    The bars
    Top left: health, hunger, thirst and energy (and air under water). Food fills hunger, drink fills thirst and sleeping in a bed fills energy; health comes back by itself when you're fed and watered. Hover them (or hold Tab) to see their %
    The body chip
    Under the bars, only when it matters: Cold, Freezing, Hot, Soaked or Filthy. Hold Tab to see how warm you feel
    J M
    Journal and map (two tabs of the pause; the same key again closes it)
    M held
    The known places nearby: the mouse (or its wheel, or a digit) picks one and letting go marks the route to it; with a route marked, the first row removes it. Esc or right click, nothing
    T or /
    Chat and commands (/help to see them). Ops (given in the server terminal with /op) have /gamemode, /tp, /give, /kill, /weather, /gamerule…; the admin also has /account, to create accounts
    Esc P
    Pause (and close windows): Q and E change tab, 1–7 jump to one; the arrows or WASD move, Enter picks, and Esc or right click goes back. In Settings, Z and C change section and R puts a row back to its default (held, the whole section). The shared world goes on meanwhile
    The character creator, the mirror and the barber's
    The arrows move and change each row, Q and E change page, Z and C held turn your character around (so does dragging it), R picks at random, Enter finishes and Esc goes back

    Up close: with relief (3D) or flat, and turn it one way or the other (a click on the rose puts north up)
    Click · right click
    Mark a route to that point (near a house, the tavern or a place, it goes to its door) · remove it. A line on the ground and on the minimap leads you there, along roads where it can, and only through land you have explored. «Share the mark» sends it to the players with you; your partner sees your route by themselves
    R N
    A mark of your own (up to 10) where the ring is. Over one of yours, R changes its drawing, R held removes it and N names it. Your partner sees them too
    L
    Index of places, the nearest first: going over one takes the map there; Enter or a click marks the route
    F
    The filters: what is shown or hidden on both maps (also with a click on the list)
    Enter X G Space
    With the keyboard: mark the route to what is in the ring, remove it, share it with those playing with you, go back to where you are
